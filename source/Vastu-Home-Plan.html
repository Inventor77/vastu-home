        Assumptions

        • Plot 24′7″ wide × 34′5″ deep, about 846 sq ft per floor. The single road runs along the front, taken as the south side.
        • Ground floor: you enter through the parking porch (SW) into the hall. Kitchen 8′ × 12′ in the SE, bedroom 12′ × 12′ on the west, pooja in the NE, both washrooms in the NW.
        • First floor: no kitchen. Lounge (11′ × 10.4′, east) opens straight onto the 8′ × 8′ road-side terrace. Bedrooms 11′ × 13′ (master, SW), 11′ × 12.5′ (west) and 11′ × 12′ (east, next to the utility), 2 washrooms, a 5′ × 4′ utility and a 6′ × 4′ open patch in the north-east.
        • Where Vastu sources disagree or this plan compromises (marked "permitted"): main door via the porch, parking in the SW, terrace on the south side.
        • Compact dog-leg stair, 5.6′ × 8.5′: 17 risers, about 7″ rise and 9″ tread, 2′9″ flights. Confirm with your structural engineer.
        • Setbacks, structure and local bylaws are not drawn. This is a concept plan, not a sanctioned drawing.
